## 1. Parties and Engagement
This Driver Logistics and Compliance Agreement ("**Driver Agreement**") is executed between:
1. **Pharmatrix Technologies Private Limited** ("**Pharmatrix**"); and
2. The enrolled logistics personnel/driver operating the Pharmatrix Driver Mobile Application ("**Driver**").

The Driver is engaged as an authorized logistics specialist responsible for the safe collection, chain-of-custody verification, and secure transit of regulated pharmaceutical waste consignments.

---

## 2. Professional Qualifications and Statutory Warranties
The Driver warrants, represents, and covenants:
1. **Valid Driving License:** The Driver possesses a current, valid commercial driving license issued by a competent Indian Regional Transport Authority (RTA/RTO). The Driver agrees to immediately notify Pharmatrix if the license is suspended, expired, or endorsed for traffic violations.
2. **Hazardous & Biomedical Waste Training:** The Driver has completed Pharmatrix’s mandatory training program on:
   - Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016 safety guidelines;
   - Proper use of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) including heavy nitrile gloves, face shields, and safety footwear;
   - Spill response protocols and emergency kit deployment;
   - Digital verification workflows (QR scan, scale weighing, collection proof photography, and OTP verification).
3. **Vehicle Fitness and Registration:** Where the Driver operates an assigned Pharmatrix vehicle, the Driver shall conduct daily pre-trip vehicle inspections (tire pressure, spill kit integrity, fire extinguisher pressure, secure cargo compartment locks).

---

## 3. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) for Collections
During active duty, the Driver must strictly adhere to the following sequence for every route stop:
1. **Arrival Check-In:** Mark arrival upon entering the geofenced boundary of the customer pickup location.
2. **QR Verification:** Scan the Customer’s generated QR token using the in-app scanner to verify correct premises.
3. **Bag Inspection & Color Coding:** Visually verify that waste bags are intact, sealed, barcoded, and segregated into authorized color streams (Yellow, Red, White, Blue, Brown). Damaged or leaking bags must be reported via the in-app exception protocol.
4. **Digital Weighing:** Place the consignment on the certified digital scale and record the measured weight in the Driver App (`collected_weight_kg`).
5. **Collection Proof Photograph:** Capture a clear in-focus photograph showing:
   - The sealed consignment with barcode visible; and
   - The scale display showing the exact weight.
6. **OTP Authorization:** Request the Customer's 6-digit OTP displayed on their Customer App. The Driver is strictly prohibited from accepting verbal or proxy OTPs without seeing the Customer's app or confirming authorized staff presence.
7. **Secure Stowing:** Immediately stow the consignment in the designated compartment of the vehicle and lock the vehicle cargo bay.

---

## 4. Location Tracking and Telemetry Disclosure
1. **Continuous Route Tracking:** The Driver expressly consents to Pharmatrix tracking the real-time GPS location of the mobile device and vehicle throughout active duty shifts (`current_latitude`, `current_longitude`, speed, and route path).
2. **Purpose of Tracking:** GPS data is used solely to:
   - Provide turn-by-turn navigation;
   - Inform waiting healthcare facilities of estimated driver arrival times;
   - Verify geofenced arrival check-ins;
   - Detect route deviations, unauthorized stops, or safety hazards;
   - Fulfill SPCB chain-of-custody tracking requirements under the BMW Rules.
3. **Shift Boundary:** Location tracking ceases automatically upon shift clock-out or terminal handover at the sorting warehouse.

---

## 5. Prohibited Conduct and Zero-Tolerance Violations
The following actions constitute material breach and grounds for immediate termination and potential criminal prosecution:
1. **Waste Diversion or Theft:** Opening, tampering with, selling, diverting, or discarding collected pharmaceutical products or waste bags.
2. **Unauthorized Handover:** Handing over waste to any facility or individual other than the authorized Pharmatrix sorting warehouse or designated CBWTF.
3. **Fraudulent Weighing:** Falsifying weights, using uncalibrated scales, or entering fictitious scale data.
4. **Bypassing Verification:** Attempting to force-complete a pickup stop without valid QR scan, photograph, or Customer OTP.
5. **Intoxication:** Operating vehicles under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or intoxicating substances.

---

## 6. Confidentiality and Privacy
- The Driver shall treat all customer names, phone numbers, addresses, and waste composition details as strictly confidential.
- The Driver shall not use customer contact information for personal purposes or share customer location photographs outside the official Pharmatrix Platform.
