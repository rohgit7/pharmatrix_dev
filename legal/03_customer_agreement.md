## Parties
This Master Customer Service Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into by and between:
1. **Pharmatrix Technologies Private Limited**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at Level 4, Healthcare Logistics Tower, Whitefield, Bangalore, Karnataka – 560066 ("**Pharmatrix**"); and
2. The commercial healthcare/pharmaceutical business entity registering on the Pharmatrix Platform ("**Customer**").

## 1. Scope of Services
Pharmatrix grants the Customer access to the Pharmatrix B2B software platform and logistics network for:
- Automated scheduling of pharmaceutical, chemical, and biomedical waste collections.
- Digital chain-of-custody tracking with QR verification and encrypted OTP handover.
- Dynamic route dispatch, weight verification, and digital receipt generation.
- Intermediate secure warehouse handling and delivery to authorized Common Bio-medical Waste Treatment Facilities ("CBWTFs").
- Issuance of statutory Form II waste handover manifests and authenticated CBWTF Destruction Certificates.

## 2. Customer Obligations and Regulatory Warranties
The Customer covenants and warrants:
- **Segregation at Source:** All pharmaceutical waste shall be segregated strictly in conformance with Schedule I of the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016 (Yellow, Red, White, Blue, Brown).
- **Authorized Packaging:** Waste must be packed in barcoded, puncture-proof, non-chlorinated containers meeting CPCB thickness and resilience standards.
- **Valid Licenses:** The Customer warrants that it holds valid retail or wholesale drug licenses under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and SPCB consents. Suspension or cancellation of such licenses must be notified to Pharmatrix within 24 hours.
- **Physical Verification:** An authorized representative of the Customer must be physically present during the scheduled pickup window to present the consignments, witness digital scale weighing, and authorize handover via the 6-digit OTP displayed on the Customer Mobile App.

## 3. Commercial Terms, Invoicing, and Taxes
- **Billing Basis:** Fees are invoiced per pickup event plus tiered weight charges based on waste category, calculated in accordance with the agreed Pricing Plan.
- **Taxes:** All charges are exclusive of statutory Goods and Services Tax (GST: CGST/SGST/IGST), which shall be charged at prevailing rates.
- **Payment Terms:** Payment is due upon invoice generation or within the agreed credit term (default: 15 calendar days). Overdue balances accrue interest at 1.5% per month.
- **Payment Method:** Electronic payment via Razorpay (UPI, Netbanking, Corporate Cards) or approved bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS).

## 4. Chain of Custody, Weight Discrepancies, and Manifests
- Handover weight recorded on the driver's calibrated mobile scale is binding unless disputed at the time of pickup.
- In the event of a variance exceeding 5% between customer estimated weight and driver scale weight, the driver scale weight prevails.
- Digital Destruction Certificates issued by partner CBWTFs are made available in the Customer Portal within 48 hours of final incineration/autoclaving.

## 5. Term and Termination
- This Agreement is valid for an initial term of one (1) year from digital acceptance and renews automatically unless terminated.
- Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon thirty (30) days' written notice.
- Pharmatrix may immediately suspend or terminate services if the Customer attempts to dispose of prohibited substances, tampers with weights, fails regulatory inspections, or defaults on payment.

## 6. Confidentiality and Data Protection
- Both parties agree to protect proprietary and commercial information.
- Personal data of pharmacists and clinic staff shall be processed in accordance with the Pharmatrix Privacy Policy and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
