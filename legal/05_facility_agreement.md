# PHARMATRIX DISPOSAL FACILITY (CBWTF) INTERFACING AGREEMENT

**Document Reference:** PMX-AGR-FAC-2026  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective Date:** Upon Facility Registration / Portal Onboarding  
**Status:** Mandatory Service Level Agreement (SLA) for Partner CBWTFs  

---

## 1. Parties and Context
This Disposal Facility Interfacing Agreement ("**Facility Agreement**") is executed between:
1. **Pharmatrix Technologies Private Limited** ("**Pharmatrix**"); and
2. The authorized **Common Bio-medical Waste Treatment Facility operator** ("**Facility**" or "**CBWTF**") operating the Pharmatrix Facility Portal.

---

## 2. Regulatory Status and Environmental Permits
The Facility warrants, covenants, and represents that:
1. It is a fully authorized Common Bio-medical Waste Treatment Facility established and operated in strict compliance with the **Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016** and the **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986**.
2. It holds valid, current **Consents to Establish (CTE)** and **Consents to Operate (CTO)** issued by the applicable State Pollution Control Board (SPCB) / Pollution Control Committee (PCC).
3. It maintains continuous online continuous emission monitoring systems (OCEMS) connected to CPCB/SPCB central servers as prescribed under CPCB guidelines.
4. Any suspension, notice of violation, or show-cause notice received from an environmental regulator shall be reported to Pharmatrix in writing within **24 hours**.

---

## 3. Shipment Receipt, Weighing, and Discrepancy Protocol
1. **Shipment Acceptance:** Upon arrival of a Pharmatrix consolidated disposal shipment (`DSP-XXXXXX`) at the Facility gate:
   - The Facility operator shall inspect the shipment manifest and seal numbers.
   - The Facility operator shall record the official received gross and tare weights on certified calibrated weighbridges (`received_weight_kg`).
2. **Discrepancy Reporting:**
   - If the Facility received weight deviates from the Pharmatrix dispatch weight (`expected_weight_kg`) by more than **2%** or **1.0 kg**, the Facility operator must log an immediate **Disposal Discrepancy** in the Pharmatrix Facility Portal stating the discrepancy reason.
   - Both parties shall conduct an immediate joint verification using intermediate warehouse intake records and driver collection proof photos.

---

## 4. Scientific Treatment and Destruction Standards
1. **Authorized Modalities:** The Facility shall process all received consignments strictly according to the designated stream:
   - **Yellow Stream (Expired Drugs & Cytotoxic):** High-temperature incineration (minimum 1050°C in secondary chamber) in accordance with CPCB standards;
   - **Red Stream (Contaminated Plastics):** Autoclaving/microwaving followed by shredding;
   - **White Stream (Sharps):** Autoclaving followed by shredding/mutilation or encapsulation;
   - **Blue Stream (Glassware):** Disinfection or autoclaving followed by glass recycling.
2. **No Secondary Resale:** The Facility covenants that under no circumstances shall any pharmaceutical product, packaging, or chemical residue received from Pharmatrix be diverted, salvaged, or resold.

---

## 5. Destruction Certificates and Manifests
1. **Form II Manifest Upload:** The Facility operator shall sign and upload the digital counterpart of the statutory Form II waste manifest into the Pharmatrix Facility Portal within **24 hours** of consignment acceptance.
2. **Destruction Certificate Issuance:** Within **48 hours** of completed scientific treatment, the Facility shall generate and upload an authenticated **Disposal / Destruction Certificate** containing:
   - Unique Certificate Number;
   - Consignment Batch & Shipment Reference (`DSP-XXXXXX`);
   - Total Treated Weight (kg) broken down by waste classification;
   - Date and Time of Incineration/Autoclaving;
   - Digital signature or stamp of the authorized Environmental Safety Officer.

---

## 6. Audit and Inspection Rights
Pharmatrix and designated environmental auditors shall have the right, upon reasonable advance notice, to inspect the Facility’s premises, weighbridge calibration certificates, incinerator logbooks, and emissions records to verify compliance with this Agreement and applicable environmental regulations.
